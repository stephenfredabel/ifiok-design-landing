Ifiok redesign: clickable preview

This is a static, clickable preview of the Ifiok storefront redesign: the homepage, the cart, and the staff Carts page. It is made from the real pages rendered with sample data, so you can judge layout, colour, light and dark themes, and the main interactions without any server. Pick a view below, then use the bar at the bottom of each page to switch views, change the window width (desktop and tablet), or flip the theme.

Views

  • Homepage

    Look at the hero and offers sliders (use the dots and arrows), the collapsible side panels, the product cards (try Add to cart on the PR2 Fixed Mug), the Show more button, and the account menu in the header.

    Open at: Desktop 1440 Tablet 1024 Tablet 768 Full window
  • Cart

    Look at the "Needs options" lines. Press Choose options, or Proceed to Checkout for the guided flow (Product 1 of 2, then 2 of 2), then a "checkout would start here" notice. Quantities and Remove work, and the totals update.

    Open at: Desktop 1440 Tablet 1024 Tablet 768 Full window
  • Staff: Carts page

    Look at the list and its filters (Open, Stale, Needs options, All), click one of the first four carts to open the details drawer, then Send reminder to see the modal. The Settings tab has the reminder switches.

    Open at: Desktop 1440 Tablet 1024 Tablet 768 Full window

Good to know

Everything here is sample data. The products, carts and customers are made-up test records.
  • Not interactive (shows a "Preview only" note): search, sign-in, checkout, payments, language and app switchers, wishlist, notifications, most links, and saving anything. Nothing is sent or stored.
  • Product photos are neutral placeholders; real photos are not part of this preview.
  • The theme follows your device setting until you change it with the Theme button in the bar.
